Loopsmith plugin

Your Loopsmith studio inside Logic, FL Studio, Ableton or any DAW. Make beats, drag sounds straight onto your tracks, and send audio from a track up to the studio, without leaving your session.

Install

  1. Unzip Loopsmith-Plugin-Mac.zip.
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) Install Loopsmith Plugin.command, choose Open, then Open again. macOS asks because the file isn't from the App Store.
  3. When it says Installed, quit your DAW completely and open it again.

Find it in your DAW

Loopsmith is an audio effect. Put it on any audio track (or your master channel to capture the whole beat). Audio passes through it untouched.

DAWWhere
Logic ProOn an audio track, click an empty Audio FX slot > Audio Units > Loopsmith > Loopsmith. If Logic says it couldn't validate it, open Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager, select Loopsmith and click Reset & Rescan Selection.
FL StudioOptions > Manage plugins, then scan for plugins. In the Mixer, click an empty insert slot on a track and choose Loopsmith.
Ableton LiveSettings > Plug-ins: turn on Use Audio Units v2 or Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders and click Rescan. Then find Loopsmith under Plug-ins in the browser and drag it onto an audio track.

Menu names can differ a little between DAW versions.

Connect it to your studio

  1. Start your RunPod machine and run start.sh as usual.
  2. Open the Loopsmith plugin window, paste the studio link into the box at the top, and click Open studio.
  3. Log in with your studio password. Each new pod link asks once.

Drag sounds onto your tracks

  1. Click the arrow button next to any sound (beats, stems, loops, one-shots, MIDI).
  2. The bar at the bottom of the plugin shows the file when it's ready.
  3. Drag that bar onto a track, the playlist or the arrangement. The file is also saved in Music > Loopsmith Library, the same folder Loopsmith Sync uses.

Download buttons inside the plugin save to your Downloads folder and show the file in Finder.

Send a track to the studio

  1. Put Loopsmith on the track you want to send (or the master for the whole mix).
  2. Click Capture this track, then press play in your DAW.
  3. Press stop when you've got what you want (up to 6 minutes). It sends automatically, and the sound opens in the studio, ready for stems, MIDI, chopping or remixing.

Click the button again while it's recording to stop and send early, or Cancel to throw the capture away.

If something goes wrong

ProblemFix
The plugin window is blank or says it can't connectCheck your RunPod machine is running and start.sh printed the link. New pod? Paste the new link and click Open studio.
Your DAW doesn't list LoopsmithQuit and reopen the DAW, and rescan plugins (see the table above). Run the installer again if needed; it also clears macOS's download flag.
The drag bar says it couldn't get the soundThe studio probably logged you out or the pod changed. Reload with Open studio, log in, and click the arrow button again.

Remove it

Delete Loopsmith.component from ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and Loopsmith.vst3 from ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.
